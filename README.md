For this project I had decided to create my parametrics with a design of using only 3 triangles. I research a lot of different font styles before deciding to do a 3 triangle design. As we only start with using A, B and C I started wuth using the 3 triangles of A. How I managed to format my parameters is by having a array of data points each having the 6 points to create one of the 3 triangles.

The reason I had decided to create 3 traingles for each parametric is because I thought it would be an interesting take on creating parametrics as they can create cool designs while also keeping visibility for users to determing what letters and numbers are what. The outcome of the letters and numbers I got whilst doing this design gives these sharp looks which I really like to my design. I can still clearly see what letter is what which was my intention for this. I think my hardest letters and numbers to do with what I am attempting was the X and the 8. The reason I think these were the hardest for me as they are usually used with more than 3 points especially the 8 as using 3 triangles to create a 8 is extremely difficult. I managed to create something for the X as I had two triangles as the bottom points for the X and the fianl triangle be the upside down triangle connecting the two triangles. This still gives the look of a X while still being unique and a sharp design. For the 8 I think this is my weakest design as it for me doesn't resemble an 8 compared to my other parametrics and for me has the weakest look, I will see if I can get a better design for the 8 but for now I will have this look to it.

For my parameters for the parametrics I used two parameters, one was the array of triangle points for the 3 triangles and the other parameter was a array storing 3 colors for each of the triangles.

For coloring for the design I took inspiration from the anime Solo Leveling where I want my parametrics to have the color theme of the main character's shadow army. His soldiers are colorized with both black and blue, and also a black and purple theme. I really like the idea of these being the colors so I had the colors for my parametrics be a black and purple look. While adding my colors it started to look with the purple like a crystal design which I started to really like as it reminds me now of looking at a ameythest or someting.

For the interpolation I used the help of chatgpt and copilot to help me with debugging and to give me ideas on how I can transition each triangle from its first point to its second point for example from A to B. With the help of ChatGPT I created a for loop which counts how many triangles there are and then creates another loop, looping the 6 points for each triangle mapping them from point A to point B in the interpolation. I also tried to make the interpolation for the colors to transition from point A to B but it appears the transition is not as smooth as I want it to be so that is something I will need to work on.
